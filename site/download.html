Requirements

  • A Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or later) on macOS 14 Sonoma or later.
  • A free Tailscale account if guests join from other devices. The app sets it up with you.
  • Free disk space for recordings. The app shows roughly how many hours you have left.
  • Guests: a recent Chrome (best), Safari or Firefox, on a computer or an iPhone.

Everything else, including the media tools used for exports, is inside the app. You don't need Homebrew, Go or Node.js.

  1. Open the disk image

    Drag p2p.video into Applications and open it. It appears in your menu bar.

  2. Connect Tailscale

    The setup checks for Tailscale and helps you sign in. It shows each command before it runs.

  3. Choose how guests join

    Private links work for people on your Tailscale network. A public link lets anyone with the link ask to join from a browser. You can turn it off any time.

  4. Run the test call

    A two-minute check of your camera, microphone, disk and connection.

  5. Start a meeting

    Copy the link, send it, let people in from the waiting room, and press record when you're ready.
